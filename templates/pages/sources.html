Data Sources

This page describes where Earnings Radar's data comes from and the freshness the project aims for. Every source is reviewed for terms and licensing before it enters production syncing. Data on this site is for personal informational use only and is not investment advice.

Where the data comes from

CapabilitySourceStatus
Companies & CIK identity SEC official company data (EDGAR) In use — SEC is the official baseline
SEC filings SEC EDGAR In use
Earnings calendar (reference layer) Alpha Vantage Free EARNINGS_CALENDAR In use as a private, personal, single-user reference layer only
Earnings calendar (canonical events) Third-party provider Provider selection and licensing pending confirmation
IR official confirmations Company investor-relations pages / feeds Implemented fixture-first; live sources pending per-source licensing review
Index constituents (S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Dow 30, Russell 2000) Official announcements / licensed vendors / controlled import Source and licensing pending confirmation

Capabilities marked pending are shown with fixture data or not shown at all until their source passes the licensing review; they are never silently filled from unreviewed sources.
